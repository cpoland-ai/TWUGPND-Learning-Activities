# C3.1 simulated inpatient patient

Faculty review draft for NFS 5103 in Fall 2, delivered through the GPND Learning Activities hub after inpatient. Use the existing NFS 5363 hospital room and EMR. The case is fictional.

## Patient and room

Elena Reyes is a 58-year-old adult admitted after abdominal surgery. Her daughter visits and can describe Elena’s usual intake and recent weight history. The RN can explain interruptions and actual delivery. The provider can clarify the surgical team's plan and when the route decision will be reassessed. The room computer opens Elena’s chart in both encounters.

## Encounter 1 · EN

- **Snapshot and notes:** The surgical team has cleared use of the enteral route. The inpatient nutrition consult requests an initial EN recommendation.
- **Orders and flowsheets:** Show route clearance, intake history, and feeding interruptions relevant to the consult. The chart leaves the target EN regimen for the student to develop.
- **Room interactions:** The patient describes tolerance. The RN clarifies barriers and interruptions to nutrition delivery. The daughter supplies baseline history.
- **Student decision:** Explain the indication and independently recommend a complete EN regimen with a rationale and monitoring priorities. Identify the chart and interview evidence that supports it.
- **Update:** A new nursing entry documents an interruption after EN starts. Ask what the student would verify and whether the new information changes the plan.

## Encounter 2 · PN

- **Same record, later hospital day:** Preserve prior orders, notes, and intake history. Add a surgical update documenting a postoperative complication and the team's decision that the enteral route is temporarily unavailable.
- **Orders, MAR, and results:** Add relevant trends and current fluid and intake documentation. The chart leaves the target PN regimen for the student to develop.
- **Room interactions:** The RN clarifies current intake and practical considerations for infusion and monitoring. The provider clarifies the expected reassessment timeline. The patient or daughter asks what changed.
- **Student decision:** Explain the route change, evaluate the proposed regimen, identify monitoring priorities, and justify one patient-specific recommendation.
- **Update:** Release one faculty-selected trend after the initial response. Ask what the student would verify, communicate, or revise.

## Build sequence

Use the chart sections already exposed by the Disease-course EMR. Create one patient record with two encounter snapshots. Finalize case values, orders, and answer anchors together before configuring PlayLab. Students export both responses as one timestamped document for Canvas. The preceptor documents actual practice evidence independently.
