NFS 5071 · Orientation

First Patient Encounter & EMR Prep

Review a fictional chart, plan your bedside questions, and practice a one-minute huddle.

Practice chart

Jordan Ellis · Hospital day 2

Admission & consult

Admitted with pneumonia. Nutrition consult requested for poor oral intake.

Diet order & intake

Regular diet. Meal record shows about one-quarter of breakfast eaten today. Lunch intake is unrecorded.

Weight history

Admission weight is 68 kg. A clinic note from two months ago lists 73 kg. Measurement conditions and whether the change was intentional are unknown.

Labs & medications

Morning chart lists creatinine 0.9 mg/dL. The medication record shows an antibiotic was administered this morning.

Nursing note

Today at 10 a.m., Jordan reported nausea and said food had tasted different since admission.

With your partner, practice introducing yourself, asking permission to talk, and comparing the chart with what the patient reports.

Give your one-minute huddle

  • What do you know from the chart?
  • What do you still need to ask Jordan?
  • What will you confirm with your preceptor?

Record key findings in your practice Nutrition Assessment Record (NAR), including each finding’s source and timing. Write unknowns as follow-up questions.

Bring your practice NAR to the debrief. This is guided practice with no graded upload. Follow the Canvas activity for the GA demonstration and class instructions.
